NAVA Tools
Flashing Firmware

Enable MIDI and then pick the MIDI out port the Nava listens on and the MIDI in port it answers on.

Hold steps 1, 3 and 5 while powering on the NAVA, select firmware and press Flash.

The device will reboot into the main operating system if no firmware has begun transferring after 30 seconds.

      DUMP patterns from NAVA
